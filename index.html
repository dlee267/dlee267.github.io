About

From physical signals to useful intelligence

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of California, Merced. My work asks how physical systems can produce better data—and how learning systems can turn that data into robust, understandable decisions.

I work across vibration and acoustic sensing, human activity recognition, foundation models, LLM-guided workflows, adaptive structures, connected health, and environmental sensing. My research combines algorithms with prototypes and field deployments in lived-in homes.
